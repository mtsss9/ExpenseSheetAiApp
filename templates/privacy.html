Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you register for Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password. As you use the Service, we also store the expense entries you log, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your account and expense data solely to operate the Service — to authenticate your account, display your expense history, and generate summaries and reports for you. We do not use your personal data for advertising, and we do not sell it to third parties.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your account and expense data are stored in the application's database and protected with reasonable technical safeguards, including password hashing. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not share your personal or financial data with third parties, except where required by law or necessary to operate the Service itself (for example, hosting infrastructure). We do not sell or rent your information to advertisers or other organizations.

5. Your Choices

You can review, edit, or delete your expense entries at any time from within the Service. If you wish to delete your account or request removal of your data, you may contact us to make that request.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and continued use of the Service after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
